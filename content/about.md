## What I do

Day to day, I work on automating systems and processes — anything from mundane
administrative tasks to complex infrastructure work. If there's a repetitive
task that's eating up someone's time, I'm probably figuring out how to script it
away.

I'm a firm believer that automation isn't just about efficiency (though that's
great too) — it's about empowering people to focus on the interesting problems
instead of getting bogged down in the routine ones.

## What you'll find here

This blog is where I share the PowerShell discoveries, solutions, and "aha!"
moments I come across. You'll find:

-   **Deep dives** into PowerShell concepts and techniques
-   **Quick tips** for those neat little tricks that make your day easier
-   **Real-world examples** from the automation trenches
-   **Explorations** of those "I wonder why that works that way" questions

I'm a lifelong learner with an insatiable curiosity about how things work under
the hood. If something piques my interest or solves a problem I'm facing, it may
well end up here.

## Beyond the blog

When I'm not writing PowerShell, you can find me contributing to open source
projects on [GitHub](https://github.com/liamjpeters). I believe in giving back
to the community that's taught me so much over the years.
